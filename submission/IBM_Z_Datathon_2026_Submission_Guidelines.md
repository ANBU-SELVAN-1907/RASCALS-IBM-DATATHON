# IBM Z Datathon 2026 – Submission Guidelines

## Submission Requirements

- No more than 3 min video that covers: the problem statement, data to support the problem, your solution, and most importantly how your solution leverages IBM Z (share your tech stack, show your LinuxONE instance and model)
- Your Code (Github link, zip upload, whatever works)

**Optional submissions:**

- Flowchart of your design
- Prototype (if available)

**Important Pointers:**

- The project title must contain Team Name. Example : [Team Name_IBM Z Datathon 2026 Project]
- Video demo of max 3 mins is compulsory
- Don't forget to add Github/Gitlab repo link

For any issues you may face while participating please use the the Issue Form give here and our team will reach out to you to resolve the issue within 48 hours - Issue Form

## Rules

- No continuation hacks are allowed; all projects must be started within the hacking time period
- All participants must be college students.
- SSF has the right to expel members/teams in violation of the rules/code of conduct.
- Taking or using any other code, projects, ideas, etc. from either participant in the hackathon or outside of it is not allowed. The project must be original and created by the participant only.
- Use of open-source libraries, frameworks are allowed but links to those resources must be added in the submission
- While a hackathon is a competition, only friendly competition is allowed. Harassing, poor sports talk, or competitive talk (ex. "I will crush you") is NOT ALLOWED.
- No projects should be created that spreads hate or bashes any social group. Avoid building projects with political inclinations.

## Project Ideas

Here are some additional project ideas (not mandatory, just some brainstorming help)

### Theme 1: Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions

- **Fraud Detection** – how can real-time AI and transactional data be used to detect suspicious transactions before they result in financial loss?
- **Healthcare Prioritisation** – how can real-time AI be used to surface time-sensitive healthcare information and support faster prioritisation of patients or cases?
- **Insurance Risk** – how can AI analyse incoming insurance claims in real time to identify potentially high-risk or unusual claims earlier?
- **Supply Chain** – how can real-time data and AI be used to detect changing supply-chain conditions and recommend timely action?
- **System Anomaly Detection** – how can AI be used to identify abnormal system activity as it happens and alert organisations before it causes a major disruption?

### Theme 2: AI Secured – Innovation Without Exposure

- **Sensitive Data Protection** – how can AI be used to protect personal, financial or other sensitive data while still enabling organisations to gain useful insights from it?
- **Cybersecurity** – how can AI be used to identify unusual activity that could signal a cyberattack or security threat?
- **Identity & Access** – how can technology be used to ensure that users access only the information they are authorised to see while identifying potentially suspicious access?
- **Secure AI** – how can an AI assistant provide useful insights while protecting sensitive information, identities and underlying systems from unnecessary exposure?
- **Quantum-Safe Security** – how can technology be used to identify cryptographic vulnerabilities and help organisations prepare critical systems and data for future quantum-era security risks?

### Theme 3: Wildcard – Beyond the Obvious

- **Accessibility & Inclusion** – how can AI and data be used to make information, services or opportunities more accessible to people with disabilities or underserved communities?
- **Education** – how can AI and data be used to identify learning challenges, personalise educational support or improve access to learning opportunities?
- **Sustainability** – how can AI and data be used to identify environmental challenges, monitor conditions or support more sustainable decisions?
- **Community Impact** – what important or overlooked problem in your community could be addressed through an innovative application of AI and data?
- **Intelligent Automation** – how can AI, including generative or agentic AI, be used to make a complex or time-consuming process more efficient?
- **Beyond the Obvious** – what problem that others may have overlooked could be addressed through a novel combination of AI, data and emerging technology?

## Judging Evaluation Criteria and Standardized Scoring Rubric

Shooting Stars Foundation will organize the IBM Z Datathon judging panels composed primarily of subject matter experts and management leaders from IBM, govern the standardized evaluation criteria with IBM, and manage the end-to-end judging process.

The judging evaluation categories and criteria are detailed below along with the standardized judge scoring rubric that is used for all global judging for the IBM Z Datathon 2026.

### IBM Z Datathon 2026 Judging Evaluation Criteria

**What Your Project Will Be Evaluated On:**

#### 1. Value to the Community & Datathon Theme Alignment

Judges will evaluate how effectively your project solution addresses a meaningful community or societal need, and how well it aligns with the current annual Datathon theme that the team selected. Consider:

- What real-world problem are you solving?
- Who benefits from your solution?
- How clearly is the event theme reflected in your idea and execution?

| 0–3 Points (Developing) | 4–6 Points (Proficient) | 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Little connection to the theme, and/or no clear community or societal need or value. | Addresses a community or society need using AI for Good, but theme alignment is minimal and value / benefit is unclear or minimal | Uses AI for Good and addresses a real-world problem with clear benefits, value and strong theme alignment. | Impactful solution to a critical need in the community or society using Tech for Good; seamless theme integration. |

#### 2. Novelty & Idea Originality

This category evaluates the creativity, originality, and uniqueness of your solution concept. Projects should:

- Present new, original ideas
- Be built entirely during the Datathon
- Feature code created from scratch only during the Datathon event timeframe

| 0–3 Points (Developing) | 4–6 Points (Proficient) | 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unoriginal concept; heavy use of pre-existing solution concepts and elements that are commonly available. | Shows creative effort but relies on common or predictable ideas. | Unique solution concept and innovative. | Highly innovative; reflects high originality and compelling creativity or is a breakthrough in concept. Shows strong end-to-end solution components. |

#### 3. Technical Design & Use of Mainframe/LinuxONE Community Cloud platform and AI /ML related open-source packages/code

Judges will review how effectively your solution leverages the Mainframe/LinuxONE Community Cloud platform and use of AI/ML related open-source packages/code. This includes:

- Strength of your technical architecture
- Use of AI/ML running on the Mainframe / LinuxONE (LinuxONE Community Cloud) platform. Two AI/ML labs will be provided and are available to participants as examples. The completion of both of the provided labs are mandatory prerequisites prior to teams building their own solution:
  - Predictive AI Hybrid AI scenarios (multi-model)
  - Predictive AI + Generative AI
- Utilization of platform capabilities such as containerization, security, scalability, and compute resources using screenshots as evidence

| 0–3 Points (Developing) | 4–6 Points (Proficient) | 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimal or no use of LinuxONE Community Cloud platform capabilities. | Basic architecture; uses LinuxONE Community Cloud platform but lacks solid use of AI/ML related open-source code/packages on LinuxONE. | Strong architecture; solid use of LinuxONE Community Cloud platform and clear use of AI/ML related open-source code/packages on LinuxONE. | Advanced architecture; optimizes LinuxONE Community Cloud platform features with exceptional exploitation of AI/ML capabilities on LinuxONE and open-source code/packages; fully exploits security and/or compute. |

#### 4. Execution & Delivery of Project Solution / Pitch Quality

Judges will look at the clarity, completeness, professionalism and solution pitch quality of your final team project video submission. This includes:

- Quality and effectiveness of your presentation
- Clarity of communication
- Compelling team pitch of your solution
- Solution demo included
- Overall team execution and delivery
- Remaining within the published time limit for video presentation submission

| 0–3 Points (Developing) | 4–6 Points (Proficient) | 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incomplete, not compelling and/or lacks clear communication. | Functional, but demonstration or communication is unpolished or does not present a compelling solution pitch. | Clear, professional submission that demonstrates good solution value and functionality, a solid solution demo, and a strong solution pitch. | Clearly demonstrates high-impact solution value and functionality, a highly professional and well-orchestrated team presentation and delivery with an excellent solution pitch, and a compelling solution demo. |

## Additional Judging Rubric for AI Innovation Excellence Awards for All Regions

The IBM Z Datathon's "AI Innovation Excellence Awards" involve an additional, more advanced judging rubric. Teams determined by the judging panels as eligible for consideration for the AI Innovation Excellence Awards have demonstrated:

- High overall scores on the Scoring Rubric for Datathon in Section 7.2 with special emphasis on high scores in the criteria dimensions of "Novelty & Idea Originality" and "Technical Design & Use of LinuxONE Community Cloud platform and AI/ML related open-source packages/code" exploitation.
- Highly novel and compelling project solutions with excellent use of AI innovation as recognized by the expert judge panels.

The AI Innovation Excellence awards do not necessarily go to the highest-scoring teams in each Datathon region. They are designed to recognize the project submissions that the Datathon judges believe represent the most compelling, out of the box, or unconventional combination of AI innovation, technical excellence, and high impact and/or transformative potential.

### Judge Scoring Rubric for AI Innovation Excellence Awards for All Regions

#### 1. AI Innovation & Originality

Evaluates the uniqueness of the AI solution, creativity of approach, and whether the team pushed beyond conventional implementations.

| 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|
| Innovative AI solution with clear differentiation and unique creative problem solving. | Truly novel, compelling, unconventional, and/or groundbreaking use of AI that significantly distinguishes the project from other submissions and demonstrates exceptional originality and problem solving. |

#### 2. Transformational Impact & Technical Excellence

Evaluates the solution's potential for real-world adoption, scalability, measurable impact, and sophistication of the technical implementation on LinuxONE Community Cloud leveraging AI.

| 7–8 Points (Advanced) | 9–10 Points (Exceptional) |
|---|---|
| Strong, advanced end-to-end technical solution with potential for high community or societal impact. | Exceptional end-to-end solution demonstrating outstanding technical sophistication, scalability, and potential to create meaningful real-world impact. |

### AI Innovation Excellence Award Score

| Category | Maximum Points |
|---|---|
| AI Innovation & Originality | 10 |
| Transformational Impact & Technical Excellence | 10 |
| **TOTAL** | **20** |
